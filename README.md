“We believe that small, mindful choices in everyday nutrition can nurture healthier lives and create a brighter future.”

MicrogreensEra began with a simple idea: bringing the goodness of fresh, thoughtfully grown greens to everyday living.

What started as a small home-grown passion soon blossomed into a meaningful journey. With a few growing trays, a deep love for clean nutrition, and a commitment to quality, Neethu began cultivating microgreens for her own family—believing that better health begins with the choices we make every day.

That humble beginning has since grown into a dedicated urban microgreens farm, bringing freshness, flavour, and mindful nutrition to households, nutritionists, fitness enthusiasts, gyms, and restaurants across Bengaluru.

For Neethu, MicroGreensEra is more than a farm—it is a reflection of her belief that nature’s smallest greens can inspire meaningful changes in the way we eat, live, and care for ourselves.

Neethu M Nair
Founder, MicrogreensEra
