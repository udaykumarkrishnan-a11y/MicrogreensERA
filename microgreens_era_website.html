Good to know

Quick questions.

What are microgreens?

Microgreens are young edible seedlings harvested after their first leaves develop. They are used as ingredients or garnishes in a wide range of dishes.

How can I eat them?

Wash and handle according to the supplier's instructions, then add them to salads, sandwiches, wraps, bowls or cooked dishes as a fresh finishing touch.

How do I know the price and availability?

Harvests and pack sizes can change. Contact MicroGreens Era for the current list, prices and delivery details.

How should I store my microgreens?

Follow the storage instructions provided with your order. Keep them refrigerated when advised and use them while fresh.
